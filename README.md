# Educational Game Scenario in Unity

## Title: DinoCity

**Target Audience:** Children aged 6 to 12  

**Educational Objectives:**  

- Develop English vocabulary  
- Learn about dinosaurs  

## Educational Component

DinoCity is an educational game aimed at enhancing English vocabulary and teaching children about dinosaurs. The game takes place in a virtual city scene, featuring a road, apartment buildings, parked cars, a park, and a small forest. The virtual city is full of surprises and learning opportunities, encouraging players to expand their vocabulary in a fun and interactive way.  

Players begin their adventure by controlling an animated character in the urban area, navigating through apartment blocks and parked cars. The character is controlled via keyboard and mouse, allowing free movement within the environment.  

Next, players reach the park, where they can explore various objects, including a playground, a lake, a green area with sandbags, and a coffee kiosk. In the middle of the park lies a small forest with hidden dinosaur statues. As players approach the statues, the dinosaur names appear in English. Pressing the "I" key displays detailed information about the dinosaur in both English and Romanian. Pressing the "C" key switches the camera to the character’s perspective.  

Players can interact not only with dinosaurs but also with other objects in the scene. Approaching an object reveals its name in English. To enhance the learning experience, pressing the "R" key shows the Romanian translation of the word, and pressing "E" returns it to English.  

The game is designed to be entertaining and engaging for children. Objects and structures in the park are realistic and colorful, and dinosaurs are represented realistically, some with animations.  

## Methodology

DinoCity employs several educational methodologies to achieve its objectives:  

- **Learning through exploration:** Players are encouraged to explore the virtual scene and discover objects and structures. This helps them learn the English names of objects and associate them with images.  
- **Learning through play:** The game is designed to be fun and engaging, motivating children to participate and learn naturally.  

## Conclusion

DinoCity encourages players to explore every corner of the city and discover English words and information about dinosaurs. Through this captivating adventure, they will learn and reinforce their English vocabulary, expand their knowledge of dinosaurs, and experience the atmosphere of an engaging virtual city. DinoCity is not just a game but an educational experience full of fun and discoveries.
